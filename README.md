# Market Insight — $7,000 Self-Learning AI Stock Model & Mutual Fund Comparator

An autonomous forward-testing stock simulation and visual dashboard built around a **$7,000** starting portfolio.

## Features
- **Daily Autonomous Updates:** Evaluates 19 stocks & ETFs every weekday after US market close using Yahoo Finance market data and live news headlines.
- **Hybrid Quantitative + Gemini AI Engine:** Combines 20d/50d moving averages, 14-day RSI swing zones, and 20-day relative strength vs. the S&P 500 with Gemini news-catalyst and risk-veto checks.
- **Self-Learning Memory (`model_memory.json`):** Audits all past trade recommendations, dynamically adjusts factor weights based on real-world win rates, applies cooldown penalties to lagging picks, and records daily lessons learned.
- **8-Option Mutual Fund & Savings Leaderboard:** Tracks how the $7,000 AI Model performs head-to-head against:
  1. S&P 500 Index Mutual Fund (`VFIAX` / `SPY`)
  2. 60/40 Balanced Retirement Mutual Fund (60% Stocks / 40% Bonds `AGG`)
  3. Nasdaq-100 Tech Growth Fund (`QQQ`)
  4. Dividend Value Mutual Fund (`SCHD`)
  5. High-Yield Savings Account (`4.50% APY` risk-free cash)
  6. Gold Bullion Fund (`GLD`)
  7. Uninvested Checking Account (`0%`)
